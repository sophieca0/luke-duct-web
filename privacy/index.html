Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Luke Commercial Kitchen respects your privacy. This page explains how we handle the details you share with us.

What we collect

Only the details you choose to give us by phone, text message or email, such as your name, contact details and information about your project.

How we use it

To reply to your message and talk through your project. We don't sell your details or share them for marketing.

How it is handled

Our website and email providers may store information outside Australia.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages and photos are viewed and which buttons are clicked; it may set cookies, and we don't send it your name, phone number or email. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The website may also save small display preferences in your browser.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, email us at lukecomkitchen@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
