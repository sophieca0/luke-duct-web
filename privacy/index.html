Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Luke Commercial Kitchen respects your privacy. This page explains how we handle the details you share with us.

Only the details you choose to give us through our contact form, by phone or by email, such as your name, contact details and information about your project.

To reply to your enquiry, talk through your project and, if you go ahead, prepare a quote and carry out the work. We don't sell your details or share them for marketing.
