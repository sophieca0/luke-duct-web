Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Luke Commercial Kitchen respects your privacy. This page explains how we handle the details you share with us.

What we collect

Only the details you choose to give us through our contact form, by phone, text message, WhatsApp or email, such as your name, contact details and information about your project.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, talk through your project and, if you go ahead, prepare a quote and carry out the work. We don't sell your details or share them for marketing.

How it is handled

Form messages are delivered to our email inbox through a trusted form service. Our website, form and email providers may store information outside Australia.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google Ads to promote our services. Google's measurement tag may set cookies in your browser so we can see whether a visit led to a call or an enquiry. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or manage ad settings at adssettings.google.com. The website may also save small display preferences in your browser.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, email us at lukecomkitchen@gmail.com.

Changes
