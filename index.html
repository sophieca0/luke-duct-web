How it works

Air Out, Fresh Air In.

Every commercial kitchen needs its exhaust and fresh air working together.

A busy commercial kitchen needs more than just an exhaust fan. Smoke, heat and grease need to be effectively removed, while fresh air needs to flow back in to keep the space comfortable.

Both systems play an important role and need to work together.

Luke's projects cover the complete setup shown in the diagram, from the wall sheet and canopy through to the exhaust fan, discharge point and fresh air supply.

Every kitchen is different, so each system is planned around the space, the cooking line and where the air can discharge.

About Luke

An Engineering Background, a Hands-On Approach.

Luke started his career with five years in civil engineering before moving into HVAC. For the past five years he has worked in mechanical ventilation, focusing on commercial kitchen exhaust and fresh air systems.

He is on site for every project shown here and leads the work alongside the team: measuring up, drawing, installing with his own hands, keeping everyone updated, and finishing with the documents, certification and as-built records.

The engineering background shows in the planning. The hands-on experience shows in the finish.
