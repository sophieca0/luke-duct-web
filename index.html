The system

Air Out, Fresh Air In.

A commercial kitchen needs a properly planned ventilation system. The exhaust removes smoke, heat and grease-laden air, while the fresh air system brings clean outside air back into the kitchen.

Both systems need to work together. We fabricate and install the complete exhaust and fresh air setup, planned to suit your kitchen and project requirements. Together, extraction and fresh air make up a commercial kitchen ventilation system.
